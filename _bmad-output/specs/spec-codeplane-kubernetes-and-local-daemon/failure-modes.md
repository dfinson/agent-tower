# Failure Modes

This companion is normative. A failure is conformant only when the externally observable state, automatic action, and recovery ownership below are all present. Stable condition names are part of operator-facing behavior.

| Failure | Trigger | Required externally observable state / condition | Required automatic action | Prohibited false-success behavior | Recovery owner | Verification fixture | Governing REQs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FM-1 SQLite unavailable | Open, fsync, WAL checkpoint, or commit cannot complete. | Readiness false; `StateStoreUnavailable`; affected operation remains pending/failed with stable retryability. | Stop authoritative mutation, projection advancement, scheduling, and publication acknowledgment; retry only within bounded policy. | Returning command success, advancing CRD state, or publishing a file reference without committed SQLite. | Control plane; storage operator if persistent. | Inject open/fsync/commit failures before and after each transaction boundary. | REQ-11, REQ-13, REQ-20, REQ-40 |
| FM-2 SQLite corrupt or integrity-chain mismatch | SQLite integrity check, WAL recovery, or AD-31 chain verification fails. | Readiness false; `StateIntegrityFailed`; installation scheduling disabled. | Fail closed, preserve evidence, enter restore/repair barrier, and prevent projection repair from corrupt authority. | Recreating state from CRDs, skipping bad history, or declaring recovery from a partial chain. | Storage/operations owner. | Corrupt page/WAL and mutate/delete/reorder chained events. | REQ-11, REQ-40, REQ-51 |
| FM-3 RWOP mount conflict | A second Pod attempts to mount the private state PVC or the original attachment is unresolved. | Control plane Pending/NotReady; `StateVolumeExclusiveMountFailed`. | Do not start a competing authority; wait for verified detach or require operator remediation. | Falling back to multi-writer access or starting with empty/new SQLite. | Cluster/storage operator. | Schedule replacement before detach and attempt a deliberate second mount. | REQ-9, REQ-11, REQ-55 |
| FM-4 RWX unavailable or stale | Mount, read-after-write, reconnect, or stale-mount monitor fails. | `SharedStorageUnavailable` or `SharedStorageStale`; affected jobs/backup publication parked. | Deny new mount grants/publication, preserve active quota, and requalify after remount before resume. | Treating stale bytes as current, acknowledging artifacts, or releasing attempts as successful. | Storage operator. | Unmount, partition, remount stale view, then restore service. | REQ-18, REQ-21, REQ-35, REQ-55 |
| FM-5 RWX non-conformant | Qualification fails fsync visibility, atomic rename, directory fsync, crash/remount durability, or `subPath`. | Installation `StorageNotConformant`; Ready false. | Block installation/upgrade activation and retain machine-readable probe results. | Warning-only install, inferred support from an access-mode flag, or use of object-like semantics. | Cluster/storage operator. | Cross-node probe with injected crash at every publication boundary. | REQ-55 |
| FM-6 Atomic publication interrupted | Writer dies before/after byte fsync, rename, directory fsync, manifest, or SQLite CAS. | Logical name remains on last verified generation; staged candidate is `Pending`, `Conflict`, or orphan inventory. | Adopt or quarantine the operation by ledger; verify bytes before any CAS or manifest completion. | Exposing partial bytes, pointer-to-missing/hash-mismatched bytes, or reporting publication success before SQLite commit. | Owning application service. | Kill writer at every authoritative, artifact, and singleton publication boundary. | REQ-20 through REQ-22, REQ-29 |
| FM-7 Orphan bytes | Bytes exist without a committed SQLite reference after crash, timeout, or abandoned upload. | Orphan inventory includes path/hash/age/class candidate; no published reference. | Quarantine and recheck references, holds, operations, and mounts before policy-driven deletion. | Auto-promoting bytes, hiding them from diagnostics, or deleting while a pending operation can adopt them. | Shared-file cleanup controller. | Seed staged/unreferenced/conflict bytes with and without pending ledgers. | REQ-24, REQ-29, REQ-43 |
| FM-8 Mirror mutation interrupted or corrupt | Git process dies during object/ref update, `git fsck` fails, or checkpoint differs. | Repository binding `MirrorIntegrityFailed`/`MirrorReacquiring`; no newer verified checkpoint. | Lock/quarantine mirror, prevent workspace/ref publication, revalidate or reacquire, then publish verified checkpoint. | Exposing a ref before objects are durable, pruning active objects, or trusting the Lease as a safety fence. | Repository-acquisition adapter. | Crash object/ref writes and inject corrupt object/ref. | REQ-26, REQ-27 |
| FM-9 Stale worker callback | Fence, Pod UID, installation incarnation, protocol, or credential no longer matches. | Callback rejected with non-enumerating stable code; stale-attempt metric/audit increments. | Transactionally reject before event append, state mutation, credential renewal, or file publication. | Returning stored success, appending a late terminal event, or accepting a second epoch. | Control plane. | Replay callbacks after cancellation, replacement, restore, and token rotation. | REQ-31, REQ-32, REQ-40, REQ-51 |
| FM-10 Worker Pod loss | Accepted Pod is deleted, evicted, crashes, exceeds deadline, or becomes unresponsive. | Attempt `Interrupted` or terminal with Pod-loss cause; job recovery policy visible. | Revoke credentials, terminate the attempt, preserve evidence, and mint a new claim generation/attempt only if policy retries. | Re-fencing the same attempt, silently recreating the Pod under the old claim, or losing active quota accounting. | Attempt controller. | Delete/evict/kill/hang worker at clone, execution, approval, and publication. | REQ-30, REQ-31, REQ-42 |
| FM-11 Node loss | Control-plane, worker, builder, or RWX-attached node disappears. | Component-specific NotReady/Interrupted plus storage attachment condition; AD-17 recovery clock visible. | StatefulSet recovery waits for RWOP safety; attempts terminate/retry under new fence; cache builders adopt/quarantine; no duplicate writers. | Claiming HA continuity, mounting RWOP twice, or preserving a worker as running without heartbeat/Pod identity. | Kubernetes controllers; cluster operator if deadline exceeded. | Hard-stop each node role with delayed detach/remount. | REQ-9, REQ-24, REQ-30, REQ-47 |
| FM-12 Cache corrupt, stale, missing, or incompatible | Manifest identity/checksum differs, generation absent, or consumer version rejects it. | Cache state `Invalid`/`RebuildRequired` or declared `ProceedingWithoutCache`. | Deny mount/use, quarantine corrupt generation, and rebuild once under identity Lease or follow explicit bypass policy. | Returning cache hit, mutating the published generation, or treating cache loss as authoritative-data loss. | Owning application/cache builder. | Alter bytes/manifest/source/config/tool identity and remove generation. | REQ-23, REQ-24 |
| FM-13 Concurrent singleton conflict | Two eligible writers publish the same logical mutable singleton from the same expected pointer. | One CAS winner; every loser is an explicit conflict candidate with provenance. | Keep committed pointer stable and require declared resolution before switching. | Last-writer-wins overwrite, deleting losing bytes, or readers observing an uncommitted candidate. | Owning application service. | Synchronize two writers around Lease expiry and pointer CAS. | REQ-20, REQ-22 |
| FM-14 Artifact missing or hash mismatch | Referenced path is absent, unreadable, or content hash differs during read, restore, import, or handoff. | `ArtifactUnavailable`/`ArtifactIntegrityFailed`; dependent job/import/restore remains blocked or explicitly degraded. | Deny consumption, preserve reference/provenance, attempt allowed recovery source, and require explicit operator/policy resolution. | Returning empty/partial content, silently rebuilding a durable artifact, or marking dependent work successful. | Artifact owner; storage operator for media loss. | Delete/truncate/substitute artifact before consumption and restore. | REQ-21, REQ-29, REQ-51, REQ-52 |
| FM-15 Backup timeout | Quiescence, writer removal, checkpoint, capture, or verification exceeds deadline. | BackupEpoch `Failed` with phase/cause; last-known-good unchanged. | Abort capture, discard/quarantine partial output, release barrier, and safely resume parked work. | Snapshot after timeout, success with a non-control RW mount, or advancing backup freshness. | Backup controller; operator for repeated failure. | Hold worker mount, operation, snapshot, and fsync beyond each deadline. | REQ-48, REQ-49 |
| FM-16 Partial or drifting backup capture | Only one volume captures, included hashes drift, manifest incomplete, or capture identity mismatches. | BackupEpoch `VerificationFailed`; partial assets inventoried but not restorable. | Reject manifest, retain previous good backup, and clean partial capture by policy. | Calling two captures atomic, accepting missing files, or using partial capture as latest restore point. | Backup controller/storage operator. | Fail RWOP then RWX capture and mutate bytes between captures. | REQ-49, REQ-50 |
| FM-17 Restore mismatch | Volume identity, manifest, chain, file hash, version, policy, or repository binding fails verification. | Restore blocked; `RestoreVerificationFailed`; scheduling remains disabled. | Preserve old/new evidence, do not rotate into active service until a complete verified set exists, and expose each mismatch. | Rebuilding authority from CRDs, activating partial jobs, or retaining old incarnation/fences after successful restore. | Operations owner. | Mix captures, alter manifest/hash, omit authoritative artifact, and replay old cursor/fence. | REQ-50, REQ-51 |
| FM-18 Direct CRD edit | User/controller changes projection spec/status outside its stable field owner. | `ProjectionDrift` condition and audit; SQLite intent unchanged. | Revert/overwrite from committed SQLite using preconditions; relist/reconcile if ownership changed. | Accepting generation as product intent, resolving a job, or granting execution from the edit. | Projection controller. | Patch every mutable-looking field and force ownership conflict. | REQ-13 through REQ-15 |
| FM-19 Egress outage versus policy denial | Gateway replicas/DNS/control dependency unavailable, or destination is explicitly denied. | Outage: `EgressUnavailable`, parked attempt. Denial: audited policy-denial outcome. | Outage pauses admission and retry budget; denial follows terminal/review policy without retry storm. | Conflating denial with outage, releasing active quota during parking, or silently bypassing gateway. | Platform operator for outage; job/operator policy owner for denial. | Kill gateway/DNS separately from denied-domain requests. | REQ-34, REQ-35 |
| FM-20 Secret rotation or invalid scope | Referenced version changes, expires, is revoked, or exceeds declared resource scope. | New admission `CredentialInvalid`/`CredentialScopeDenied`; active attempt shows retained-version or restart decision. | Reject broad/invalid mount; bind new attempts to new version; complete or explicitly cancel/restart active attempts. | Hot-swapping an active secret invisibly, persisting value, or continuing with revoked/broader credential. | Security owner; control plane enforces. | Rotate/revoke/broaden secret during active and queued attempts. | REQ-32, REQ-36 |
| FM-21 Upgrade incompatibility | CRD/schema/layout/protocol preflight finds unsupported version, active floor, conversion, or migration state. | Upgrade blocked before mutation with explicit compatibility report. | Drain or retain N-1 attempts, resume ledgered migration, rollback within verified window, or require restore/forward-fix. | Partial contract, ordinary Helm CRD rollback claim, destructive downgrade, or backup during pointer switch. | Release/cluster operator. | N/N-2 protocol, incompatible schema, crash every migration phase, webhook/CA outage. | REQ-54, REQ-56 |
| FM-22 Cancellation in a major phase | Cancel arrives while queued/admitted, cloning, tool execution, waiting approval, publishing artifact/handoff, reviewing, or mutating a ref. | Durable `Canceling` phase and final outcome identify where cancellation occurred and whether required output publication failed. | Execute REQ-42 ordering, fence late callbacks, preserve recovery bundle/evidence, and finalize exactly once. | Immediate success before stop/publication decision, lost approval, partial artifact presented as complete, or remote ref mutation without recovery evidence. | Cleanup orchestrator plus owning phase service. | One fixture for each listed phase, including repeated cancel and controller restart. | REQ-28, REQ-31, REQ-42 |
| FM-23 Cleanup or finalizer blockage | Pod, mount, Lease, workspace, artifact hold, external resource, or storage deletion cannot complete. | Resource remains `CleanupBlocked` with owner, phase, retry count, age, and retained inventory. | Retry boundedly, preserve tombstone/evidence, alert on age, and require audited break-glass for force-finalize. | Removing finalizer while responsibility remains, claiming resource deleted, or deleting held/referenced bytes. | Named finalizer controller; cluster administrator for break-glass. | Block each responsibility, restart N/N-1 controller, then exercise safe force-finalize. | REQ-29, REQ-42 through REQ-44 |
| FM-24 Approval or delegation authorization failure | Actor lacks role/scope, delegation is expired/revoked/version-mismatched, policy generation changed, or actor/service attempts self-approval. | Stable denied approval condition and immutable audit identify actor, effective role, target, policy generation, and non-secret reason. | Reject in the same SQLite transaction that revalidates authorization; leave approval/job intent unchanged and require a newly authorized decision. | Accepting from cached authorization, racing revocation, self-approval, or returning prior success for a different actor/policy digest. | Policy/authorization service; security owner for binding correction. | Race expiry/revocation/policy update against commit and exercise every role/resource/self-approval denial. | REQ-14, REQ-37, REQ-40 |
| FM-25 Stream replay degradation | Cursor incarnation/scope/hash is invalid, replay window compacted, retained history absent, client is slow, or stream disconnects. | Stable cursor rejection, `replay_window_exceeded`, explicit snapshot/retention condition, or deliberate slow-client disconnect without resource existence disclosure. | Preserve authoritative history, direct eligible clients to bounded replay or snapshot, and converge to current SQLite sequence/hash with at-least-once duplicates allowed. | Silent event drop, cross-job/install oracle, treating keepalive as history, or claiming continuity across an unverifiable gap. | History/SSE service; client performs replay/snapshot protocol. | Disconnect and resume at every emission/compaction boundary with expired, prior-incarnation, wrong-scope, slow, duplicate, and out-of-order cases. | REQ-6, REQ-15, REQ-41 |

## Failure Traceability Gate

Release qualification MUST fail if any FM row lacks its named fixture, externally observable condition, automatic action assertion, prohibited-behavior assertion, recovery owner, or linked passing REQ evidence.

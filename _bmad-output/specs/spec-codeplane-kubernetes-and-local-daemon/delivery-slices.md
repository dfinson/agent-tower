## Slice Gate Rules

1. Each slice MUST retain the prior slices' evidence and MUST leave local-daemon conformance green.
2. An intentional difference MUST be an asserted product outcome, never a skipped shared test.
3. A slice MUST NOT advance when any in-scope failure fixture can produce false success, an unowned recovery path, or an unclassified shared file set.
4. CodeRecon indexes and session-handoff flows MUST remain representative fixtures of generic cache/artifact/shared-file behavior.
5. Epic/story decomposition SHOULD follow these slice boundaries; a story may be smaller but MUST identify its REQ and failure-fixture coverage.
